How to choose the right release control

1. Identify the release step

Source/merge, build/test, store packages, approve, provision, deploy, shift traffic, or recover?

2. Identify the target

EC2 fleet, ECS task set, Lambda alias, Beanstalk environment, or CloudFormation stack?

3. Identify the boundary

Host availability or request percentage? Merge approval or release approval? Another account, another Region, or a private VPC?

CodeBuild builds and tests. CodePipeline orchestrates. CodeDeploy deploys and shifts supported traffic. CodeArtifact stores packages.
