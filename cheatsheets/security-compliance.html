3. Direct cross-account permission combinations

Caller permission + resource-owner permission. If KMS encryption is involved, also unlock the key.

OperationCaller IAM role policyResource-owner policies
Read S3 objectss3:GetObjectS3 bucket policy allows the source role
Decrypt with another account's KMS keykms:DecryptKMS key policy authorizes source-account use (Q34)
Read SSE-KMS S3 objectss3:GetObject + kms:DecryptBucket policy + customer-managed KMS key policy (Q30)
Read a KMS-encrypted secretsecretsmanager:GetSecretValue + kms:DecryptSecret resource policy + KMS key policy (Q23: options 1, 2, 3)
S3 = Role + Bucket. KMS = Role + Key. Encrypted S3 = Role + Bucket + Key. Encrypted secret = Role + Secret + Key.

These are direct cross-account patterns with no overriding denies. Assuming a role in the destination account is a different pattern. Ordinary same-account S3 access does not always require a bucket policy.

Policy placement: IAM policy = what I may do; bucket/secret policy = who may access me; key policy = who may use this key. A bucket policy cannot grant kms:Decrypt.
