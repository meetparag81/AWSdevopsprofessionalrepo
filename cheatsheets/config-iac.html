How to choose the right IaC tool

1. Identify the lifecycle

Author, review, provision, configure at runtime, maintain OS state, detect drift, or recover a failure?

2. Identify the scope

One resource, a stack, a managed-node fleet, an OU, or multiple accounts and Regions?

3. Identify the guarantee

Prevent, detect, repair, retain, validate, wait for readiness, or roll back?

Choose by the lifecycle and required control, not just the word "configuration".

Option numbers preserve the supplied reference key, not official AWS exam answers. Some keys are incomplete, ambiguous, or outdated: the amber notes state the practical correction. Do not memorize claims of instant updates or automatic repair without checking the mechanism.
